LEVEL CALIBRATION

Calibrate input levels

StreamLine measures your source and picks the ADC attenuation for you. Takes about a minute. You’ll need:

  1. Your source connected to the line input and powered on
  2. Something loud to play — the most dynamic track you have nearby

Levels change live while it runs — streaming keeps going. Cancelling puts your current settings back.

First, measure the quiet

Leave everything connected, but pause playback on your source. StreamLine listens for a few seconds to learn its idle level.

Now play the loudest track you have

Starting from 0 dB, StreamLine raises the attenuation until loud passages stop clipping.
